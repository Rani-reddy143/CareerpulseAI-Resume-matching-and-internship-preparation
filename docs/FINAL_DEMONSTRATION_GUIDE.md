## Step-by-Step Evaluation Protocol

### Step 1: Candidate Profile Management
1. Navigate to the **"My Profile"** tab.
2. View the candidate details for **Rani Reddy** (B.Tech CSE, IIT, 8.9 CGPA).
3. Observe education entries, skills wizard, experience at TechPulse Innovations, projects, and career preferences.

### Step 2: Resume Upload & Intelligence Parsing
1. Navigate to **"Resume Upload"**.
2. Click **"Load Sample PDF Resume"** or drag-and-drop a sample file.
3. Click **"Run AI Resume Extraction Engine"**.
4. Observe the automatic transition to **"Resume Intelligence"** showing parsed skills, confidence scores (Skills: 96%, Education: 99%), and candidate summary.

### Step 3: Internship Matching & RAG Discovery
1. Navigate to **"Internship Matches"**.
2. Observe ranked internship recommendations powered by the RAG vector engine.
3. Review transparent compatibility scores (e.g. 88.5%), required skill alignments, and natural language match explanations.

### Step 4: Skill Gap Analysis & Employability Pathway
1. Navigate to **"Skill Gap"** or click **"Analyze Skill Gap"** for an internship posting.
2. View the **Readiness Score** (e.g., 53.5%) and 5-category gap breakdown.
3. Review the 2-week learning roadmap and explanations for why each missing skill matters for daily duties.

### Step 5: Application Customization (Resume & Cover Letter)
1. Navigate to **"Cover Letter"**.
2. Select target internship.
3. Observe the generated role-specific cover letter and tailored resume bullets.
4. Verify the **Authenticity Guardrail** badge confirming 100% truthfulness and zero hallucinated experiences.

### Step 6: Interview Preparation & Mock Drill
1. Navigate to **"Interview Prep"**.
2. Generate 5 categories of technical, behavioral, and system design questions.
3. Submit a sample written answer in the **Mock Interview Simulator** to receive real-time numerical scoring (0-100) and feedback.

### Step 7: Application Tracking & Management (M4.1)
1. Navigate to **"Application Tracker"** (or click **"Track Application"** directly on any internship match card).
2. View the **Dashboard Overview Cards** (Total Applications, Active Pipeline, Upcoming Deadlines, Scheduled Interviews, Offers, Rejected).
3. Switch between **Kanban Board View** and **List View**.
4. Click **"Manage"** on an application to:
   - Change stage status (e.g. to "Interview scheduled").
   - Add interview dates, times, and Google Meet links.
   - Attach generated cover letters and tailored resume summaries.
   - Write application notes and manage deadline reminders.

### Step 8: Live Conversational Assistant
1. Open the floating **AI Career Assistant Drawer** (bottom right) or navigate to **"AI Career Assistant"**.
2. Type queries such as:
   - *"Find suitable software internships for me"*
   - *"What are my skill gaps for TechPulse?"*
   - *"Help me prepare for my technical interview"*
3. Observe accurate intent classification, agent routing, context preservation, and grounded responses.
